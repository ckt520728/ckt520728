Clinical nephrologist and cognitive neuroscientist building reproducible tools at the intersection of **neural dynamics, biosignal analysis, medical AI, and agentic research workflows**.

My public repositories combine scientific investigation with practical software: research workspaces, reusable AI skills, educational applications, and published knowledge products. Private clinical repositories and identifiable research data are intentionally excluded from this public index.

## Repository architecture

```text
Research questions and clinical problems
                 |
                 v
     Incubators and research workspaces
       2026Cordex | 2026-Antigravity | 2026-Hermes
                 |
        promote reusable components
                 v
         Canonical skills and methods
                claude-skills
                 |
          build and publish products
                 v
 Applications | teaching tools | research resources | lab website
```

The intended ownership model is simple:

- **Workspace repositories** hold experiments, orchestration, session records, and promotion manifests.
- **`claude-skills`** is the canonical home for reusable AI skills and research workflows.
- **Product repositories** own deployable application code.
- **`kidney-cognition-lab`** is the public publication layer for articles, visualizations, and teaching material.
- Forks are retained as clearly separated upstream references, not presented as original work.

## Research themes

- **Computational and neural dynamics** — recurrent networks, FORCE/RLS learning, slow manifolds, fixed points, oscillations, and stimulation-response dynamics.
- **EEG and biosignal machine learning** — signal processing, cognitive-state characterization, and longitudinal neurocognitive assessment.
- **Cognition, dementia, and healthy aging** — visual short-term memory, neuropsychological assessment, and cognitive-training design.
- **Clinical and medical AI** — reproducible analysis workflows, evidence synthesis, clinical safety boundaries, and privacy-aware tooling.
- **Agentic research engineering** — multi-agent orchestration, verification loops, reusable skills, and research-to-publication pipelines.

## Repository standards

Across maintained projects, I am moving toward:

- one canonical source for each reusable component;
- explicit separation of source code, generated outputs, publications, and private data;
- automated tests, type checks, dependency audits, and GitHub Actions;
- deidentified or synthetic data in code repositories;
- citation-ready research documentation and reproducible build instructions.
