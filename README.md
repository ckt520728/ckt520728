## 🔬 Research Focus & Scientific Domains

- **Computational & Neural Dynamics**:
  - Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) membrane potential ODE models & Basal Ganglia (STN-GPe) network interactions.
  - Parkinson's Disease limit cycle attractors & Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) resonance dynamics.
  - FORCE (First-Order Reduced-Controlled Error) learning algorithm with online RLS synaptic adaptation and slow manifold fixed-point analysis.
- **Biosignal & Medical Machine Learning**:
  - Machine Learning & Deep Learning analysis on EEG biosignals.
  - Early classification & longitudinal tracking of Dementia (Normal Elderly vs. Alzheimer's / Dementia).
- **Agentic AI & Medical Tooling**:
  - Designing open-source medical AI skill suites ([OpenClaw Medical Skills](https://github.com/ckt520728/OpenClaw-Medical-Skills)).
  - Loop engineering, agentic workflow automation, and multi-agent coordination for scientific discovery.
